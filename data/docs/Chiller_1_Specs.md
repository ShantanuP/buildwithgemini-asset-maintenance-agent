# Technical Specifications: York YK Centrifugal Chiller (CHILLER-01)

## Equipment Overview
* **Model:** York YK Centrifugal Liquid Chiller (300-Ton Capacity)
* **Asset ID:** CHILLER-01
* **Location:** Basement Mechanical Room
* **Application:** Central Chilled Water Loop for Whole Building Cooling

## Operating Parameters & Standard Specs
* **Chilled Water Flow Rate:** 720 GPM (Gallons per Minute)
* **Evaporator Entering/Leaving Water Temp:** 54°F / 44°F (Design Delta T: 10°F)
* **Condenser Water Flow Rate:** 900 GPM
* **Condenser Entering/Leaving Water Temp:** 85°F / 95°F
* **Refrigerant Type:** R-134a
* **Oil Type:** York Type K Synthetic Chiller Oil
* **Compressor Type:** Open-Drive Single-Stage Centrifugal

## Maintenance Protocols & Troubleshooting
1. **Chilled Water Temperature Drift / Short Cycling:**
   * Calibrate dual thermistor temperature sensors on evaporator inlet/outlet.
   * Verify chilled water flow switch functionality and pressure drop across vessel.
2. **Preventive Maintenance Interval:**
   * Spectrographic Oil Analysis & Filter Change: Annually.
   * Evaporator & Condenser Tube Eddy Current Testing: Every 3 years.
