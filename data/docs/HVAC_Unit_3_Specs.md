# Technical Specifications: Trane IntelliPak 50-Ton Rooftop HVAC Unit (HVAC-03)

## Equipment Overview
* **Model:** Trane IntelliPak 50-Ton Packaged Rooftop Unit
* **Asset ID:** HVAC-03
* **Location:** North Tower Roof (4th Floor Access)
* **Application:** Comfort Cooling & Ventilation for Floors 3 and 4

## Operating Parameters & Standard Specs
* **Supply Airflow:** 17,500 CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute)
* **Refrigerant Type:** R-410A (Factory Charge: 48.5 lbs)
* **Design Suction Pressure:** 118 – 130 psig
* **Design Discharge (Head) Pressure:** 340 – 380 psig
* **Operating Supply Temp Range:** 52°F – 58°F (Target Cooling Supply: 54.5°F)
* **Electrical Input:** 460V / 3-Phase / 60Hz (MCA: 98A, MOP: 110A)
* **Filter Spec:** 24" x 24" x 2" MERV 13 High-Efficiency Pleated Filters (Quantity: 8)

## Maintenance Protocols & Troubleshooting
1. **High Head Pressure Trip (>420 psig):**
   * Check condenser coil for debris or dirt accumulation. Flush coils with non-acidic cleaner.
   * Verify condenser fan motors are operational (RPM check).
   * Check thermal expansion valve (TXV) bulb contact and insulation.
2. **Preventive Maintenance Interval:**
   * Filter Inspection & Change: Every 90 days.
   * Compressor Oil & Refrigerant Pressure Test: Every 180 days.
   * Fan Belt Inspection & Tensioning: Every 90 days.
